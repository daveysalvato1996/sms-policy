SMS Privacy Policy

Salvato Advisory · last updated 19 August 2026

This policy covers the text messages sent by Salvato Advisory, a sole proprietorship operated by David Salvato in Raleigh, North Carolina. It covers nothing else.

What the messages are

One short text per business day containing a summary of that day's figures from a single auto repair business: money collected, the sales tax included in that total, amount billed, number of cars completed, and month-to-date billing. Recipients are the owners of that business, who asked to receive it.

Message frequency

Approximately one message per recipient per business day — about 22 messages per month. Frequency may vary.

Mobile numbers are never shared

Mobile phone numbers collected for these text messages are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, for any purpose. They are used only to deliver the messages described above. Numbers are given to us directly by the recipient and are held only for as long as that person wishes to receive the messages.

Message delivery is carried out by Twilio, our messaging provider, acting solely on our behalf in order to transmit the message. No other party receives the number.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on the recipient's own mobile plan and are set by their carrier, not by us.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. A recipient may also simply ask us directly and we will remove them.
