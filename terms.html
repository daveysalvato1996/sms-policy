The service

Salvato Advisory sends a short daily business summary by text message to the owners of one auto repair business, at their request. The message contains that day's money collected, the sales tax included in it, amount billed, cars completed, and month-to-date billing.

Who receives it

Only people who have asked for it, verbally and directly, and who have given us their own mobile number for this purpose. We do not collect phone numbers from the public and we do not add anyone without asking first.

Frequency

Approximately one message per recipient per business day, about 22 messages per month. Frequency may vary.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Any charge is set by the recipient's own mobile carrier under their own plan. We charge nothing.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP at any time for help. A recipient may also ask us directly and we will remove them.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. See our SMS Privacy Policy.
