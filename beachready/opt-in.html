How customers sign up for texts

Beach Ready Auto Repair (JM Automotive LLC) · 8863 Caratoke Highway, Point Harbor, NC 27964 · last updated 23 October 2026

Beach Ready Auto Repair texts only its own customers and people who contact the shop first, from (252) 489-4149. There is no purchased, rented or imported list. Message frequency is about 2 messages per visit. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

1. At the service counter (verbal consent)

When a customer books, the service advisor asks, word for word:

Want us to remind you the day before your appointment, and let you know when your vehicle's ready? We'd send a text and an automated call from (252) 489-4149 -- that's about 2 messages per visit, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP any time to cancel.

The advisor waits for a yes or no. A yes is recorded with the staff member's name, the date and the wording used, and the customer is sent this confirmation:

Beach Ready Auto: you're set up for appointment reminders and ready-for-pickup alerts from (252) 489-4149 -- save it so we don't land in your spam. About 2 messages per visit. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Terms & privacy: https://daveysalvato1996.github.io/sms-policy/beachready/

2. The customer texts the shop first

Replies come in the same thread, from staff or from the shop's automated assistant, which says in its first reply that it is automated, asks about the vehicle and the job, and passes the request to the service advisor. It sends at most 6 replies in a conversation the customer starts, and never marketing. Its first reply reads like this:

Beach Ready Auto: you've got the shop's automated assistant, we're closed till [day] at [hour]. What are you driving and what's it doing? STOP to opt out.

A customer who has texted the shop also gets the appointment reminder and vehicle-ready texts about their own visits, about 2 per visit. Every message names the shop and says Reply STOP to opt out.

3. The customer calls and the automated phone assistant answers

The assistant's greeting says the call may be recorded and transcribed. Before the call ends it asks:

Want me to text you what you told me? It's one text from (252) 489-4149, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to opt out.

Only if the caller says yes, it sends one text confirming what it took down. If they say no, nothing is sent:

Beach Ready Auto: thanks for calling. We have down: [what]. The service advisor will text you to set it up. STOP to opt out.
