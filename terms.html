The service

Salvato Advisory sends a short daily business summary by text message to the owners of one auto repair business, at their request. The message contains that day's money collected, the sales tax included in it, amount billed, cars completed, and month-to-date billing.

This is an operational account notification about the recipient's own business. It is not marketing, it is not promotional, and no goods or services are advertised, offered or sold in these messages. No message is ever sent on behalf of any other party.

Who receives it, and how they opt in

Only named individuals who have asked for it directly and who have given us their own mobile number for this purpose. We do not collect phone numbers from the public, there is no web form, and nobody is added without being asked first and agreeing.

Consent is given directly to David Salvato, in person, by phone or in writing, after the recipient has been told what the messages contain, how often they arrive, that message and data rates may apply, that they can reply STOP at any time, and that these Terms and the SMS Privacy Policy apply. The opt-in wording used is:

"I'd like to text you a short summary of your shop's numbers at the end of each business day — money collected, sales tax included in it, amount billed, cars completed, and month-to-date billing. It's about one message per business day, roughly 22 a month. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop them, or HELP for help. By saying yes you agree to the SMS Terms and Conditions and the SMS Privacy Policy at daveysalvato1996.github.io/sms-policy. Would you like me to start sending it to this number?"

A record of each recipient's agreement, with the date, is kept by Salvato Advisory.

Frequency

Approximately one message per recipient per business day, about 22 messages per month. Frequency may vary.

Message and data rates may apply. Any charge is set by the recipient's own mobile carrier under their own plan. We charge nothing.
