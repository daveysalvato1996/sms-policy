SMS Privacy Policy

Beach Ready Auto Repair · last updated 19 October 2026

This policy covers the text messages and automated phone calls sent by JM Automotive LLC, doing business as Beach Ready Auto Repair, 8863 Caratoke Highway, Point Harbor, North Carolina 27964. It covers nothing else.

What the messages are

Two kinds of message about a vehicle the recipient has brought to us, or is booked to bring to us:

  • An appointment reminder, sent the day before a booked appointment.
  • A ready-for-pickup notice, sent when work on the vehicle is finished.

And texts to a person who has contacted the shop first, by calling or texting its number:

  • One text after a call nobody could answer, asking what the caller needs.
  • One text after a call the shop's automated phone assistant answered, confirming what it took down.
  • Replies when the person texts us back. These may come from the shop's automated assistant rather than a person. Its first reply says it is automated. It asks about the vehicle and the job and passes the request to the shop manager. It sends at most 6 replies in a conversation the person starts.

These are operational service notifications about the recipient's own vehicle and their own appointment. They are not marketing and they are not promotional. Nothing is advertised, offered or sold in them, and they never contain a price, an offer or a recommendation to buy anything.

Automated calls

Where a customer has agreed to it, the same two notices may also be delivered as an automated (prerecorded) phone call to the same number. The call says the same thing as the text and asks for nothing.

Message frequency

About two messages per visit: one reminder before the appointment and one notice when the vehicle is ready. Most customers bring a vehicle to us two or three times a year, so most recipients receive fewer than ten messages a year. Frequency varies with how often a customer visits. When a person contacts us first: one text after a call, and if they text back, at most 6 replies in that conversation plus at most one later note.

How we get a number, and how consent is given

A mobile number is given to us by the customer, directly: at the counter or on the phone when they book, or by texting the shop from it. There is no web form and no sign-up page, and we do not buy, rent or import phone numbers.

When we ask, we tell the customer what they will receive and wait for a yes. We keep a dated record of each customer's agreement, including the wording they were read.

Since 5 October 2026, a customer whose number has texted the shop also gets the appointment reminder and ready-for-pickup texts from (252) 489-4149 without being asked first. These are informational texts about that customer's own appointment or vehicle, not marketing: about two per visit, one the day before a booked appointment and one when the vehicle is ready. Every one says "Reply STOP to opt out." Replying STOP, or asking in plain words such as "please stop", ends them for that number. They are texts only: an automated call still needs the customer's yes.

The texts after a call, and the replies to a person's own texts, go only to the number that just called or texted us.

Mobile information is never shared for marketing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Mobile phone numbers collected for these messages are not sold or rented, and are used only to deliver the messages described above.

Information may be passed to subcontractors providing support services, strictly in order to deliver the messages, write the automated assistant's replies and pass replies to the shop:

  • Twilio, our messaging provider, which sends and receives these texts, and places the automated calls, on our behalf from the shop's texting number (252) 489-4149. It receives the number and the words of each message, and keeps its own record of the messages it carries.
  • Railway, which hosts the shop's automated assistant. It receives the person's number, the words of the conversation and the note the assistant writes for the shop. It also receives a short list, refreshed every minute, of numbers that have texted the shop in the last few hours or have asked us to stop, so that the assistant stays out of those conversations, and it holds a copy of the shop's customer records, which the assistant checks the number against.
  • Anthropic, whose AI model writes the automated assistant's replies (while the shop is trying the assistant out, replies are written but not sent). It receives the words of the conversation and whether the number belongs to a customer the shop has on file. It is not given the person's name, their customer record or the number the text came from.
  • Vonage, the shop's phone company, which carries the shop's main line (252) 489-4891. Replies to our texts are passed to that line so that staff can read them, so it receives the number and the words of the reply.

This is a service-delivery function, not sharing for marketing. None of these providers is given the information for marketing, and we do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers for marketing.

The automated assistant's own copy of a text conversation is deleted after 30 days. A request to stop is kept for as long as it applies, so that it is honoured. The note the assistant passes to the shop is kept with the shop's other records for that customer.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on the recipient's own mobile plan and are set by their carrier, not by us. We charge nothing for these messages.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. A customer may also simply tell us, in the shop or by calling 252-489-4891, and we will remove them. A text asking us in plain words to stop texting is treated as a request to stop: the automated assistant stops at once and passes it to the shop to record.

Stopping the messages does not affect the service on the vehicle in any way.
