The service

JM Automotive LLC, doing business as Beach Ready Auto Repair, 8863 Caratoke Highway, Point Harbor, North Carolina 27964, sends its own service customers two kinds of notice about their own vehicle:

  • An appointment reminder the day before a booked appointment.
  • A ready-for-pickup notice when work on the vehicle is finished.

Where the customer has agreed to it, the same two notices may also be delivered as an automated (prerecorded) phone call to the same number.

It also answers people who contact the shop first, by calling or texting its number. Those texts are described below, under Texts when you contact us first.

These are operational service notifications about the recipient's own vehicle. They are not marketing, they are not promotional, and no goods or services are advertised, offered or sold in them. They never contain a price, a quote, a discount or a recommendation to buy anything. Every message is sent by Beach Ready Auto Repair about its own work, on its own behalf.

Who receives it, and how they opt in

Two groups of the shop's own customers receive these notices:

  • Customers of Beach Ready Auto Repair who have been asked and have said yes, and who have given us their own mobile number for this purpose. Only this group can receive the automated call.
  • A customer whose number has texted the shop, for the two texts only. See If you have texted the shop below.

There is no web form and no online sign-up. We do not buy, rent or import numbers. Nobody receives these notices unless they said yes when we asked or their number has texted the shop.

When we ask, consent is taken in two steps, and the second step is what carries the full disclosure.

1. We ask, in the shop or on the phone, when the customer books:

"Want us to remind you the day before your appointment, and let you know when your vehicle's ready? We'd send a text and an automated call from (252) 489-4149 -- that's about 2 messages per visit, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP any time to cancel."

2. If they say yes, the first thing we send is this confirmation, before any reminder is ever sent:

"Beach Ready Auto: you're set up for appointment reminders and ready-for-pickup alerts from (252) 489-4149 -- save it so we don't land in your spam. About 2 messages per visit. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Terms & privacy: https://daveysalvato1996.github.io/sms-policy/beachready/"

A dated record of each customer's agreement is kept, including the wording they were read and the number it applies to.

If you have texted the shop

Since 5 October 2026, a customer whose number has texted the shop also gets the appointment reminder and ready-for-pickup texts from (252) 489-4149 without being asked first. These are informational texts about that customer's own appointment or vehicle, not marketing: about two per visit, one the day before a booked appointment and one when the vehicle is ready. Every one says "Reply STOP to opt out." Replying STOP, or asking in plain words such as "please stop", ends them for that number. They are texts only: an automated call still needs the customer's yes.

Texts when you contact us first

These texts go only to a person who has just called or texted the shop's number themselves. Nobody else receives them.

  • After a call nobody could answer: one text asking what you need.
    "Beach Ready Auto: sorry we missed your call. What can we help you with, and which vehicle is it about? Just reply to this text. STOP to opt out."
  • After a call the shop's automated phone assistant answered: one text confirming what it took down.
    "Beach Ready Auto: thanks for calling. We have down: [what]. The service advisor will text you to set it up. STOP to opt out."
  • When you text us back: replies may come from the shop's automated assistant rather than a person. Its first reply says it is automated. It asks about your vehicle and what it needs, and passes your request to the shop manager, who contacts you to book it. It does not book appointments, quote prices or promise times. It sends at most 6 replies in a conversation you start, and at most one short note if you text again after that. Its first reply reads like this:
    "Beach Ready Auto: you've got the shop's automated assistant, we're closed till [day] at [hour]. What are you driving and what's it doing? STOP to opt out."

Words in [brackets] are filled in for each message. If you ask us to stop in your own words (for example "please stop texting me"), the automated assistant stops at once and passes your words to the shop so the request is recorded. STOP and HELP are always answered by our messaging service, never by the assistant.

Frequency

About two messages per visit: one before the appointment, one when the vehicle is ready. Most customers bring a vehicle to us two or three times a year, so most receive fewer than ten messages a year. Frequency varies with how often a customer visits.

When you contact us first: one text after a call, and if you text back, at most 6 replies in that conversation plus at most one later note.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Any charge is set by the recipient's own mobile carrier under their own plan. We charge nothing.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP at any time for help. A customer may also tell us in the shop or call 252-489-4891, and we will remove them.

Stopping the messages does not affect the service on the vehicle in any way.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
