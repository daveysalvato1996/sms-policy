SMS Privacy Policy

Beach Ready Auto Repair · last updated 27 August 2026

This policy covers the text messages and automated phone calls sent by JM Automotive LLC, doing business as Beach Ready Auto Repair, 8863 Caratoke Highway, Point Harbor, North Carolina 27964. It covers nothing else.

What the messages are

Two kinds of message about a vehicle the recipient has brought to us, or is booked to bring to us:

  • An appointment reminder, sent the day before a booked appointment.
  • A ready-for-pickup notice, sent when work on the vehicle is finished.

These are operational service notifications about the recipient's own vehicle and their own appointment. They are not marketing and they are not promotional. Nothing is advertised, offered or sold in them, and they never contain a price, an offer or a recommendation to buy anything.

Automated calls

Where a customer has agreed to it, the same two notices may also be delivered as an automated (prerecorded) phone call to the same number. The call says the same thing as the text and asks for nothing.

Message frequency

About two messages per visit — one reminder before the appointment and one notice when the vehicle is ready. Most customers bring a vehicle to us two or three times a year, so most recipients receive fewer than ten messages a year. Frequency varies with how often a customer visits.

How we get a number, and how consent is given

A mobile number is given to us by the customer, directly, at the counter or on the phone when they book, after we have asked them whether they want the reminders and told them what they will receive. There is no web form and no sign-up page: nobody is added without being asked first and saying yes. We do not buy, rent or import phone numbers, and we do not add existing customers to the programme without asking them.

We keep a dated record of each customer's agreement, including the wording they were read.

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Mobile phone numbers collected for these messages are not sold or rented, and are used only to deliver the messages described above.

Information may be passed to subcontractors providing support services strictly in order to deliver the message — specifically Twilio, our messaging provider, which transmits the message on our behalf and uses the number for no other purpose. This is a service-delivery function, not sharing for marketing, and no other party receives the number.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on the recipient's own mobile plan and are set by their carrier, not by us. We charge nothing for these messages.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. A customer may also simply tell us — in the shop, or by calling 252-489-4891 — and we will remove them.

Stopping the messages does not affect the service on the vehicle in any way.
