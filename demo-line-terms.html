The service

The Salvato Advisory demo line, toll-free (855) 907-3585, lets business owners try an AI assistant that answers calls and texts for a made-up example shop. Every message says it is a Salvato Advisory demo. No goods or services are sold in these messages and none is sent on behalf of another party.

Who receives messages, and how they opt in

The number is given only to business owners who asked to see the product. A person opts in by texting the line first, which starts the conversation, or by calling it: during the call the assistant asks "Want me to text you a summary of this call?" and sends one text only if the caller says yes. That yes is kept with the call recording. No one is texted who has not contacted the line.

Frequency

Only in reply to a person who contacted the line; usually a few messages per conversation. Frequency may vary.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, set by the recipient's own carrier. We charge nothing.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See the Demo Line SMS Privacy Policy.
