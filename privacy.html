SMS Privacy Policy

Salvato Advisory · last updated 20 August 2026

This policy covers the text messages sent by Salvato Advisory, a sole proprietorship operated by David Salvato in Raleigh, North Carolina. It covers nothing else.

What the messages are

One short text per business day containing a summary of that day's figures from a single auto repair business: money collected, the sales tax included in that total, amount billed, number of cars completed, and month-to-date billing. Recipients are the owners of that business, who asked to receive it.

These messages are operational account notifications. They are not marketing, they are not promotional, and nothing is being sold or advertised in them.

Message frequency

Approximately one message per recipient per business day — about 22 messages per month. Frequency may vary.

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Mobile phone numbers collected for these text messages are not sold or rented, and are used only to deliver the messages described above. Numbers are given to us directly by the recipient and are held only for as long as that person wishes to receive the messages.

Information may be passed to subcontractors providing support services strictly in order to deliver the message — specifically Twilio, our messaging provider, which transmits the message on our behalf and uses the number for no other purpose. This is a service-delivery function, not sharing for marketing, and no other party receives the number.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on the recipient's own mobile plan and are set by their carrier, not by us.

Stopping the messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. A recipient may also simply ask us directly and we will remove them.
