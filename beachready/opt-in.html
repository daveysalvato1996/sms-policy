How customers sign up for texts

Beach Ready Auto Repair (JM Automotive LLC) · 8863 Caratoke Highway, Point Harbor, NC 27964 · last updated 22 October 2026

Beach Ready Auto Repair texts only its own customers and people who contact the shop first, from (252) 489-4149. There is no purchased, rented or imported list. Message frequency is about 2 messages per visit. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

1. At the service counter (verbal consent)

When a customer books, the service advisor asks, word for word:

Want us to remind you the day before your appointment, and let you know when your vehicle's ready? We'd send a text and an automated call from (252) 489-4149 -- that's about 2 messages per visit, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP any time to cancel.

The advisor waits for a yes or no. A yes is recorded with the staff member's name, the date and the wording used, and the customer is sent this confirmation:

Beach Ready Auto: you're set up for appointment reminders and ready-for-pickup alerts from (252) 489-4149 -- save it so we don't land in your spam. About 2 messages per visit. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Terms & privacy: https://daveysalvato1996.github.io/sms-policy/beachready/

2. The customer calls the shop first

If nobody answers, the caller gets one text asking what they need:

Beach Ready Auto: sorry we missed your call. What can we help you with, and which vehicle is it about? Just reply to this text. STOP to opt out.

If the shop's automated phone assistant answers, the caller gets one text confirming what it took down:

Beach Ready Auto: thanks for calling. We have down: [vehicle and problem]. The service advisor will text you to set it up. STOP to opt out.

Nothing more is sent unless the customer replies.

3. The customer texts the shop first

Replies come in the same thread, from staff or from the shop's automated assistant, which says in its first reply that it is automated, asks about the vehicle and the job, and passes the request to the service advisor. It sends at most 6 replies in a conversation the customer starts, and never marketing.

A number that has texted the shop may also receive the appointment reminder and vehicle-ready texts about its own visit, about 2 per visit. Every message names the shop and says Reply STOP to opt out.
