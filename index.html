Summary

Salvato Advisory sends one short operational account notification per business day to the two owners of a single auto repair business, at their own request. Each message contains that day's figures for their own business. These messages are not marketing and not promotional, nothing is sold or advertised in them, and no message is sent on behalf of any third party.

Recipients opt in verbally and directly. There is no web form and no public list, and numbers are never collected from the public. The opt-in wording is reproduced in full in the Terms and Conditions.

Approximately 1 message per recipient per business day (about 22 per month). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.
